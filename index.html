Who we are

Driven by quality, trusted by builders.

Since 1991, Supreme Steel Pipe Corporation has been manufacturing black iron and galvanized iron steel pipes for the Philippine construction, industrial, and infrastructure market.

Our mission here at Supreme Pipe goes beyond producing quality steel pipes. We build the foundations of progress. Guided by integrity, innovation, and commitment, we aim to strengthen industries, communities, and the nation through reliable and sustainable solutions.
